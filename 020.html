INTERNAL SOLID EQUILIBRIUM

Internal Stress Resultants

CROSS-SECTIONAL EQUILIBRIUM

Stress States

External forces induce distributed internal stress components ($\sigma, \tau$) to satisfy continuous material integrity.

Axial Tension ($N > 0$)

Collinear normal forces extending the longitudinal axis with Poisson lateral contraction.

Axial Compression ($N < 0$)

Collinear normal forces shortening the axis with transverse lateral expansion.

Pure Bending ($M$)

Force couple inducing curvature, creating simultaneous compression and tension zones across the neutral axis.

Transverse Shear ($V$)

Tangential transversal forces inducing sliding distortion across adjacent planes.

RIGID BODY MECHANICS

Static Equilibrium & Determinacy

2D EQUILIBRIUM EQUATIONS

Fundamental Conditions

To prevent rigid body kinematics in a 2D plane ($x, y$), support reactions must satisfy three scalar conditions:

$$\sum F_x = 0 \quad \Big| \quad \sum F_y = 0 \quad \Big| \quad \sum M_z = 0$$

Degree of Determinacy ($N_r \text{ vs } N_e$)

Unstable / Mechanism ($N_r < N_e$)

Insufficient constraints. The structure experiences unconstrained rigid body motion under load.

Statically Determinate / Isostatic ($N_r = N_e$)

Reactions are uniquely determined using equilibrium equations alone without kinematic displacement formulations.

Indeterminate / Hyperstatic ($N_r > N_e$)

Redundant constraints enhance structural safety and stiffness, requiring compatibility equations to solve.
